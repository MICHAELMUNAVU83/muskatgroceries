Meet Mary — the mama mboga who went digital.

Muskat Groceries started with a simple determination: make a grocery business work, even when the traditional route wasn't working.

  • Mama Mboga, but digital
  • Built through TikTok

Founder Mary Muthoni took her business online, built a community through TikTok, and kept finding new ways to serve her customers. Her journey was recognised by KEPSA with the Female Rising Star in Digital Innovation Award.
